Inside the MR-Series

Scroll to take the arm apart.The arm, part by part.

01

Base and axis 1

Bolted to the floor or a riser, or to a wall or ceiling. Wall mounting limits the axis 1 range. Axis 1 swings the whole arm.

02

Shoulder, axis 2

Lifts and lowers the lower arm. Each of the six axes has its own servo drive.

03

Lower and upper arm, axis 3

Together they set the reach to the wrist center: 1,440 mm, 2,080 mm or 2,185 mm, by model.

04

Wrist, axes 4 to 6

On the welding models the wrist is hollow, so the torch cable runs inside it. Axis 6 turns at up to 700°/s.

05

Tool flange

A torch, gripper or cutting head mounts here. Its weight counts in the rated payload. Repeatability is ±0.05 mm to ±0.08 mm, by model.

Questions

What plant managers ask us.

Where are MR-Series robots assembled?
MR6-1400, MR6-2080 and MR50-2185 are assembled, integrated and tested at our facility in Riyadh Second Industrial City before they ship. The other models in the range are supplied to order. Core components and platform technologies come through an established international partnership. Assembly, wiring, testing, integration and service are done by Maknana in Riyadh.
Can I order any model in the range?
Yes. We quote the model your cell needs. For a model supplied to order, the quotation confirms its values and the delivery date. The trial or the study tells you which model fits your part.
Are MR-Series robots collaborative robots (cobots)?
No. They are industrial robots with four or six axes, for guarded cells. Each cell is risk-assessed to ISO 12100. It is guarded by fences, interlocked doors, light curtains and emergency stops. The robot safety standards are ISO 10218-1 and ISO 10218-2.
What does the rated payload include?
Rated payload at the flange, including the gripper, within the wrist load diagram. On the MR50-2185 this means bags up to about 25 kg with a light gripper.
Can I buy just the robot?
Yes. A standalone robot comes with commissioning and training, for customers with their own integration team. The integrator then owns the cell risk assessment and guarding (ISO 12100, ISO 10218-2).
What is the Saudi Made mark?
MR6-1400, MR6-2080 and MR50-2185 carry the Saudi Made mark. Price preference in government tenders follows the local-content rules for each tender; your procurement team can confirm what applies to your project.
