Trial in Riyadh

Bring us your part. Where a cell matches, see it run in Riyadh.

Where a ready cell matches your part, it runs your part while you watch. If your part needs a special cell, we start with a study: a cell layout and a simulation.

Request a trial Call us
Two ways to start

Start with a trial, or with a study when your part needs a special cell.

1

What you send

  • Photos or a drawing of the part
  • The work it needs: welding, cutting, bending or handling
  • Its material, size and weight
  • Your yearly volume

We sign an NDA on request.

2

What happens in Riyadh

  • We check whether a cell at our facility matches your part.
  • We tell you what we prepare before your visit, and what the trial will show.
  • You bring the part, or samples of it.
  • The cell runs your part while you watch.

We confirm that a suitable cell is available before you travel. For a small-change package, the cell may run a part close to yours.

3

What follows

  • An offer for the package: standard, or with the change your part needs.
  • We build the cell and test it on your part in Riyadh before it ships.
  • You can witness that factory acceptance test.
  • One contract covers the cell. A service plan is agreed with it.

Ask us which cells are available for a trial.

1

What you send

  • One part you make again and again: welded, cut, bent or handled
  • Its drawing or a photo
  • Your yearly volume
  • Your cycle time and crew today, if you have them

We sign an NDA on request.

2

What comes back

  • A cell layout around your part
  • A simulation of the cell at work
  • The cycle time for your part

We agree the study date with you.

3

What follows

  • You approve the concept at a second visit, then receive a priced offer.
  • We build the cell and test it on your part in Riyadh before it ships.
  • You can witness that factory acceptance test.
  • One contract covers the cell and the agreed cycle time.

Simulated figures are estimates until a timed run on your part.

Questions

What plant managers ask before a trial.

Which cells can I see running?
It depends on what is available at our Riyadh facility. Send your part details and we confirm a suitable cell before you travel.
What if no ready cell matches my part?
Then we start with a study. You receive a cell layout, a simulation and an estimated cycle time for your part.
Who sees my drawing?
Only the engineers who work on your part. If you want an NDA, we sign one on request.
What if I make many different parts?
Start with the one that repeats most. A cell can take a family of similar parts, and we show you which ones fit.
Which robot will the cell use?
We size the robot to your part: payload with the gripper, reach and cycle time. You buy a complete cell, and the robot is one part of it.
