Press brakes, and robots that tend them.

CNC press brakes from our range. Robot tending of the brake is assessed per part in the trial or the study.

What changes

The same work, before and after the cell.

Today
  • Heavy or long blanks need two people at the brake.
  • Long blanks are slow and tiring to handle, and positioning against the back gauge varies.
  • Hands work close to the closing punch and die.
With an automatic cell
  • Where the part suits a robot, it holds the blank through every bend on the same programmed path.
  • Operators program, check angles and prepare stacks.
  • People stay outside the guarded cell while the ram moves.
What the solution includes

You buy the whole cell, with the equipment around the robot.

Depending on your part, the cell is standard, standard with a small change, or special.
