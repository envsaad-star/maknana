What changes

The same work, before and after the cell.

Today
  • Hand-held (semi-automatic) MIG/MAG: arc on for about 45% of working time (TWI typical).
  • Penetration varies from welder to welder and day to day.
  • Output depends on how many skilled welders are on shift.
  • Welders breathe fume classified as carcinogenic to humans (IARC Group 1).
With an automatic cell
  • Automatic MIG/MAG: arc on for about 90% of working time (TWI typical), about twice the hand-held figure.
  • The same programmed path, speed, torch angle and welding parameters on every part; welds are checked by inspection.
  • Operators set up, inspect and run the cell.
  • People stand outside the guarded cell, away from the arc and fume.

Questions

What plant managers ask us.

Do I need to redesign my part?
Usually not. The trial or the study checks access for the torch, fixturing and fit-up tolerance, and tells you if a small change to the part would make the cell faster.
Who programs the robot?
We do, during commissioning, and we train your team to change programs and add parts. Software support continues under your service plan.
Does it work for short batches?
Yes, if the parts are similar. Fixtures and stored programs make changeovers quick; the trial or the study shows the changeover time for your mix.
What happens to my welders?
They can move to set-up, inspection, programming and the welds that still need a person. On a customer's tank line, 8 welders a shift became 3 operators.
How are the welds checked?
Welds are checked by visual testing (VT). Penetrant (PT), magnetic particle (MT), radiographic (RT) or ultrasonic (UT) testing follows as specified. Acceptance follows ISO 5817 or AWS D1.1, as the customer specifies. Welding procedures can be qualified to ISO 15614-1.
How is the cell guarded?
Each cell is risk-assessed to ISO 12100. It is guarded by fences, interlocked doors, light curtains and emergency stops. The robot safety standards are ISO 10218-1 and ISO 10218-2.
What does a welding cell cost?
We quote after the trial or the study. The price depends on your part, so the offer states it.
