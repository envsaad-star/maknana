Overview

This fiber laser cutting system cuts steel sheet, and in one version also tube. It comes as a sheet-only machine or a combined sheet-and-tube machine. Both have ±0.02 mm positioning repeatability and a 120 m/min running speed.

Features

  • Fast processing: maximum running speed of 120 m/min and 1.5 G acceleration.
  • Flexible power range, from 1,000 W to 12,000 W, for different material thicknesses.
  • Dual capability: the sheet-and-tube version cuts flat plate and round or square tube without switching machines.
  • Less material waste, thanks to the narrow kerf and precision of fiber laser cutting.
  • Positioning repeatability of ±0.02 mm (maker's figure).

Applications

Suits sheet metal fabrication, structural steelwork, and workshops that process both flat plate and tubular steel on one platform.

What is a CNC steel plate cutting machine?

A CNC steel plate cutting machine uses a computer-controlled fiber laser to cut steel sheet. Combined versions also cut round and square tube. The CNC program guides the laser along the cutting paths. This gives clean edges with little manual set-up or rework.

Maintenance

Routine maintenance keeps cutting accuracy and uptime high. Key tasks:

  • Clean and inspect the laser cutting head and protective lens regularly.
  • Check that the fiber laser source and cooling system work properly.
  • Verify voltage stability and electrical connections (380 V, 50/60 Hz).
  • Lubricate the guide rails and, on sheet-and-tube models, the tube clamping mechanism.
  • Test positioning repeatability periodically to confirm it stays within ±0.02 mm.

Choosing the right machine

The choice between the sheet-only and the sheet-and-tube configuration depends on your material mix:

  • Sheet only suits shops that work only with flat steel plate, with a working area of 3,050 × 1,530 mm.
  • Sheet and tube suits shops that also cut round tube (ø20–ø350 mm) or square tube (20–245 mm). No separate tube cutter is needed.
  • Match laser source power to your usual plate thickness: higher power within the 1,000–12,000 W range cuts thicker material.
  • Check that the running speed and repeatability (120 m/min, ±0.02 mm) fit your volume and part tolerances.
