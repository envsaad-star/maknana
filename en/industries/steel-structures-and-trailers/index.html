The cell

A welding cell built around your longest part.

The integrator answers for building, programming and safety-integrating the cell.

  • Robot: MR6-2080, 2,080 mm reach, for long seams. A gantry version on a guide rail welds longer parts.
  • Seam finding: Seam scanning finds joints; vision checks part position and weld presence.
  • Part holding: Fixtures, or a positioner that turns the part.
  • Welding: Power source, torch and wire feeder, set for your material and thickness.
  • Safety: Fences, interlocked doors, light curtains and emergency stops.

No manual point teaching: a 3D scan finds the seams and the software generates the weld paths. Weld sizes still come from the customer's drawing or welding procedure.
